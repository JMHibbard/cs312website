Resources

Here are some resources that I have found helpful in my research on bird classification using machine learning. These include bird identification tools, datasets for training models, and research references related to audio and image classification.

Bird Identification

  • All About Birds — Cornell Lab of Ornithology’s guide to bird species across North America
  • eBird — A citizen science platform for logging bird sightings and exploring range maps
  • Audubon Bird Guide — Field guide to birds of North America with photos and calls

Machine Learning Datasets

  • Kaggle Bird Species Dataset — A large image dataset used for training bird classification models
  • Xeno-canto — A community database of bird sound recordings from around the world
