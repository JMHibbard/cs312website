Here are some resources that I have found helpful in my research on bird classification using machine learning. These include bird identification tools, datasets for training models, and research references related to audio and image classification.

Bird Identification

  • All About Birds : Cornell Lab of Ornithology's guide to bird species across North America. This will be useful for identifying different species and understanding their behaviors in a general sense and help as an entry point for learning more about the birds I will be oberving
  • eBird : A citizen science platform for logging bird sightings and exploring range maps. This will be useful for tracking bird populations and understanding their distribution and migration patterns
  • Audubon Bird Guide : Field guide to birds of North America with photos and calls. This will be useful for identifying different species and eventually deviations in typical call and song patterns

Machine Learning Datasets

  • Kaggle Bird Species Dataset : A large image dataset used for training bird classification models
  • Xeno-canto : A community database of bird sound recordings from around the world
